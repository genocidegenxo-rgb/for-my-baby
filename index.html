sometimes i wish you could borrow my eyes for just a moment and see yourself the way i see you because i genuinely do not think you understand what you have become to me

you came into my life and somehow reached places inside my heart that i had spent so long keeping hidden from everyone else i never imagined that i would let somebody this close to me or care about somebody so deeply that the thought of a future without them could make my entire world feel unfamiliar and yet here i am loving you in ways i never knew my heart was capable of

before you i thought i understood what it meant to love somebody but you have taught me that love is so much more than saying those three little words love is remembering the smallest things about you love is wanting to understand you even when you struggle to understand yourself and love is staying gentle with somebody even when life has given them every reason to build walls around their heart

and baby i want you to know that you never have to be a perfect version of yourself for me to love you

you do not have to wake up happy every morning you do not have to hide your tears or apologize for having difficult days you do not have to pretend that everything is okay just because you are afraid that your sadness might become too much for me

i want the real you my love the girl who laughs until she cannot breathe and the girl who overthinks everything at three in the morning the girl who sometimes feels like the entire world is against her and the girl who still finds a way to keep going even when she is tired i want to know the thoughts you are afraid to say out loud and the feelings you struggle to explain and i want to be somebody you can turn to when you no longer have the strength to carry everything by yourself

you are not a burden to me diana you are not a problem i need to solve and you are not somebody whose love i will suddenly stop wanting because life has become difficult you are my girl and i want to learn how to love you through every season of your life

if your walls go back up i will not punish you for being afraid if you need time to breathe i will give you room without making you question whether i still love you and if your mind starts telling you that you are difficult to love i will keep reminding you that you do not have to earn the place you already have in my heart

i cannot promise that i will always know the perfect thing to say but i can promise that i will keep trying to understand you i will listen when i have misunderstood you and i will take responsibility when my actions hurt you because i never want my love to become another thing you have to recover from i want to be the kind of love that allows you to put your guard down without being afraid of what happens next

and god baby if you could see the future i sometimes imagine when i think about you i imagine a little home somewhere in this enormous world where the kitchen smells like something we cooked together and your favorite flowers sit somewhere they can catch the sunlight i imagine a kitchen island where we sit together late at night talking about absolutely nothing while somehow making those ordinary moments feel like the most important memories of our lives

i imagine stealing kisses from you while you are trying to do something and pulling you into my arms when you pretend that you are annoyed with me i imagine waking up beside you and realizing that the girl i used to miss through a screen is finally close enough for me to kiss her forehead and whisper good morning without having to say goodbye through a phone

i imagine holding your hand when we go somewhere together and looking at you when you are not paying attention because even after all that time i know there will still be moments when i look at you and wonder how i became lucky enough to call you mine

i want the ordinary days with you just as much as i want the beautiful ones i want the grocery store trips and the sleepy mornings and the long conversations and the nights when neither of us knows what to talk about but neither of us wants to hang up i want the little disagreements we learn to work through and the apologies that bring us closer and the years that slowly turn our memories into stories we tell each other over and over again

i do not only want you when everything is romantic and easy i want to build something with you that can survive the days when life is neither romantic nor easy because to me you are not a temporary feeling or a beautiful chapter i am afraid to finish you are the person i want beside me when the chapters become difficult too

sometimes i wonder how a person can live an entire life without knowing that somebody like you exists somewhere in this world how many sunsets i watched before i knew that one day i would have somebody whose existence could make the future feel worth looking forward to

if i could travel back through every lonely night every difficult year and every version of myself that believed he would always have to keep his heart to himself i would tell that boy to keep going because one day he would meet a girl who would make him want to believe in forever again

i would tell him that one day he would find somebody whose voice could soften the hardest parts of his day somebody whose happiness would become precious to him somebody he would want to protect without ever making her feel trapped and somebody he would want to love so carefully that she would never have to wonder whether she mattered

and if the universe gave me a thousand lifetimes i would spend every single one searching for you i would look for you in crowded cities and quiet little towns i would search for you beneath unfamiliar skies and across oceans i would recognize something in you before i understood why you felt familiar and i would fall in love with you in a thousand different ways until every lifetime had become another story about how my heart found its way back to yours

maybe that is what you have become to me baby a kind of home that does not depend on a place or a perfect moment but exists wherever we are able to find each other

i know that distance can make some days feel impossibly long and i know that loving somebody you cannot always hold can leave your arms missing a person your heart already knows by memory but i need you to understand that the miles between us have never made you less important to me

you are still the girl i want to tell about my day you are still the person i want to hear from when something good happens and you are still the person whose happiness i want to celebrate even when i cannot be there to kiss your cheeks and hold you close until the day i can hold you for as long as i want i hope my words can reach the places my arms cannot

i hope you feel loved when you read this on a difficult night i hope you remember that somewhere there is a boy who sees a future with you and who wants to turn his promises into actions one day at a time and i hope that whenever your mind tries to convince you that you are not enough you remember that you never needed to become anybody else to be worthy of being loved

you are my diana exactly as you are

and if there is one thing i wish i could place inside this little flower for you to carry forever it would be the certainty that my love is not measured by how easy a day is or how perfectly we understand each other it is measured by the care we choose to give each other the honesty we choose when things become difficult and the countless little decisions we make to protect something that matters to us

i want to keep choosing you in those little ways i want to keep learning you even after i think i know everything i want to keep asking about your dreams even when we have been together for years i want to keep making you laugh when the world has been unkind to you and i want to keep reminding you that being loved does not mean you have to hide the parts of yourself that are still healing

and when we are old enough to look back at the lives we built i hope we can sit beside each other and smile at how young we were when we first started dreaming about forever i hope i can look at you then with your hand in mine and tell you that every difficult day we survived together was worth it because i would still choose the same girl i chose when all we had were words and a screen and a thousand miles between our hands

i would choose you with the same heart and with even more reasons

so this flower is for you my love it is a tiny little reminder that beautiful things can grow slowly that even the most delicate petals deserve patience and that love does not have to bloom perfectly every single day to remain beautiful

and if one day these petals fade i hope you remember that the love behind them was never meant to be something temporary you are my favorite thought in the middle of an ordinary day my quiet wish before i fall asleep my most beautiful reason to believe in tomorrow and the person i hope to find beside me when tomorrow finally becomes our life together

i love you beyond the words i know how to write and beyond the distance i wish i could erase and beyond every little flower i could ever give you

if i had to begin this entire life again with no memories of who you were or how we found each other i would still hope for you and if the universe asked me what i wanted from all the time it could possibly give me i would not ask for a perfect life or a world without difficult days i would ask for enough time to love you properly

enough mornings to wake up beside you enough evenings to hold you close enough years to watch you grow into every version of yourself you have ever dreamed of becoming and enough lifetimes to keep discovering new reasons why the girl i fell in love with was always worth finding

because if forever is made of moments then i want mine to be made of you your hand in mine your head resting against my chest your laughter filling a room we call home your flowers growing beside a window and your sleepy voice telling me that you love me when the whole world has finally gone quiet

that is the kind of forever i dream about not a perfect story but a real one with you in every chapter not a promise that life will never hurt us but a promise that we will keep learning how to love each other through it not a love that asks you to become somebody else but a love that reminds you that the person you already are is somebody worth choosing every single day

my diana my beautiful girl if i could give you the whole universe i would still want to give you something more i would want to give you the ability to see yourself through my eyes just once so you could understand why i am so grateful that you exist and why even in a universe full of beautiful things there is nobody i would rather come home to than you

and until i can give you all the mornings and sunsets and quiet little moments i dream about i will give you what i can today my patience my honesty my effort my tenderness my loyalty and a heart that wants to keep learning how to love yours better

you are my first thought when i dream about the future and the person i want beside me when those dreams become real you are my girl my favorite soul my sweetest home and my most beautiful what if that became my most beautiful reality

and if there really are a thousand lifetimes waiting somewhere beyond this one then save me a little place beside you in every single one because no matter how many times the world begins again no matter how many stars disappear and no matter how many flowers bloom beneath unfamiliar skies i will always hope that somewhere in that endless universe there is a girl named diana waiting to take my hand
